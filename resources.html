  • 2. For best accuracy, ensure the image is shown full screen on a screen matching the pattern's resolution.
  • 3. On larger screens, display the image at true dimensions (100% size, no scaling).

Printable Calibration Patterns

Directions:
  • 1. Download the pattern for your desired print size (use right-click and "Save link as..." in case it opens in browser).
  • 2. Print the pattern at 100% scale (no scaling, "actual size" or "no fit to page") to ensure correct dimensions.
  • 3. Use a high-quality printer, smooth surface and rigid backing for best results.
