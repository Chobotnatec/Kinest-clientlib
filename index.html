Kinest

Augmented Reality Tracking for Educational Robots

What is Kinest?

Kinest is an Android application for tracking educational robots using ArUco markers. It enables spatial awareness through real-time AR pose estimation.

Features

  • Detects ArUco markers in real time
  • Overlays 3D orientation and position
  • Designed for use in STEM classrooms
  • Works offline, minimal setup

How It Works

Place printed ArUco markers in your environment. Point your device’s camera and watch the app detect, localize, and track each robot.

Get Started

Download Kinest from the Google Play Store and begin exploring AR robotics instantly.
